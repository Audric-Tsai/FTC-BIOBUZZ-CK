**Notes on each option (row above, in order):**

1. **REV 75mm Mecanum Wheel Set** — Lower-profile wheel (75mm vs 96mm), which frees up more chassis clearance/space for intakes and other mechanisms underneath the robot. Steel-plate construction with bearing-supported rollers, and it's a "native" fit since it's built for the REV DUO 5mm hex shaft system you already have everything else in (no adapters to source separately). Good pick if your drivetrain design is tight on vertical space.

2. **goBILDA 96mm Mecanum Wheel Set (70A Durometer)** — Larger diameter gives more ground clearance and slightly better traction/rollover of field bumps and game elements, and 70A durometer rollers are a common favorite in the FTC community for grip. goBILDA's mecanum line has a long track record and lots of build documentation/community support. Good pick if ground clearance matters more than compactness for your game strategy.

**Recommendation:** Pick ONE of these two sets, not both — they do the same job (4 wheels = one complete mecanum drivetrain) and mixing diameters would make the robot tilt/bind. If you're unsure, 96mm is the more common/proven choice across FTC teams; 75mm is the pick if you specifically need the extra clearance underneath the robot.
